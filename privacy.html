Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2026

This policy describes what we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

Information we collect

We collect limited information needed to connect families and providers, including contact details, ZIP code, and care preferences. Providers may submit business details such as name, phone, website, and service location.

How we use information

We use this information to match families with nearby hospice, palliative, or home care providers and to notify providers of new client requests.

Disclosure of information

We share client contact details and non-sensitive care preferences with matched providers so they can respond quickly. We do not sell user data.

Payments

Provider subscription payments are processed securely by Stripe. Best Hospice does not store full payment card details.

No PHI

Best Hospice does not collect or store medical records, insurance IDs, Social Security numbers, or other protected health information (PHI).

Security practices

We use industry-standard safeguards, access controls, and secure transport (HTTPS) to protect data. Access to provider dashboards is protected by authentication.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at support@besthospice.com.
