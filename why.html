Meet the Team

We’re a mission-driven team built on character, respect, and service.

President

Crawford Outland

Founder of Best Hospice and Home Health, focused on product vision, operations, and strategic direction. Best Hospice and Home Health was created from a deeply personal, mission-driven experience after witnessing my family navigate the challenges of hospice care firsthand.

I was raised in a home where character, respect, and service to others were foundational values. From a young age, I was taught the importance of integrity, humility, and treating every person with dignity. These principles guided me beyond my hometown to attend IMG Academy, where discipline, accountability, and respect for others shaped both my academic and athletic development.

Driven by a long-standing desire to serve, I attended the United States Military Academy at West Point, where character, leadership, and selfless service were rigorously developed. Following graduation, I completed Ranger School and served as an Officer and Platoon Leader in The Old Guard, leading ceremonial missions at Arlington National Cemetery. In that role, I was entrusted with honoring our nation’s heroes and supporting their families during moments of profound loss, an experience that deeply reinforced the importance of compassion, precision, and respect.

Throughout my military career, I observed my own family struggle to navigate hospice care for loved ones. Those experiences revealed a clear need for a simpler, more transparent way for families to connect with reputable hospice providers. Best Hospice and Home Health was built to meet that need—helping families during one of life’s most difficult transitions.

Best Hospice and Home Health reflects my continued commitment to service: supporting families with clarity, dignity, and care. As Charles Dickens wrote, “No one is useless in this world who lightens the burdens of another.”

Because your loved ones deserve the best, period.
