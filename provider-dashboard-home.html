Privacy & Billing Notice

Best Hospice and Home Health collects only the information needed to deliver lead notifications and manage your subscription. Your data is used to notify you of client leads, to bill your account, and to operate your provider dashboard. We do not sell your data. Information may be shared with service providers (e.g., payment processing) solely to complete these functions, and is protected with appropriate security practices.

For full details, please review our Privacy Policy and Billing Terms in the billing portal or contact support if you have questions.
