Finding Hospice Care in Glendale, AZ

Glendale is the fourth-largest city in Arizona and the hub of the West Valley. Its diverse neighborhoods include established communities near downtown Glendale, the Arrowhead area to the north, and connections to the Sun City retirement communities — among the largest planned retirement communities in the country. Hospice providers serving Glendale typically extend their coverage throughout the West Valley, including Peoria, Surprise, Avondale, Litchfield Park, and Goodyear.

Banner Thunderbird Medical Center is the primary acute care hospital for the West Valley and is located in Glendale. It has an active palliative care consultation program and experienced case managers who facilitate transitions to home hospice when curative treatment is no longer the patient's goal. The large retirement population in adjacent Sun City and Sun City West creates strong demand for experienced hospice teams in this corridor.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Glendale patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost. Covered services include nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program and the ALTCS long-term care program cover hospice for qualifying low-income residents. Dual-eligible patients typically have all hospice costs covered. Arizona has no Certificate of Need requirement, giving Glendale families a wide selection of hospice agencies.
