Finding Hospice Care in Fort Mohave

Fort Mohave is an unincorporated community in the Mohave Valley along the Colorado River in the far western corner of Arizona. The area — which includes Fort Mohave, Mohave Valley, and neighboring Bullhead City — sits across the river from Laughlin, Nevada and close to Needles, California. It is a distinctly tristate community, and many services including healthcare cross state lines routinely.

The area has a large population of retirees and long-term residents who have settled along the river for the warm climate and outdoor lifestyle. Western Arizona Regional Medical Center (WARMC) in Bullhead City is the primary hospital for the area. Hospice agencies serving Fort Mohave may be based in Arizona, Nevada, or California — confirm that any provider you consider holds current Arizona licensure and Medicare certification for services delivered on the Arizona side.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Fort Mohave patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost. Coverage includes nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program covers hospice for qualifying low-income Mohave County residents. Dual-eligible patients typically have all hospice costs covered. Note that if your loved one is enrolled in a Nevada or California Medicaid plan, eligibility rules may differ — contact your plan to confirm coverage for care received in Arizona.
