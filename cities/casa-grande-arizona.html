Finding Hospice Care in Casa Grande

Casa Grande sits at the geographic midpoint of the Phoenix-Tucson corridor, approximately 60 miles south of Phoenix and 70 miles north of Tucson along Interstate 10. The city serves as the commercial hub for Pinal County and the surrounding agricultural communities of Coolidge, Eloy, Maricopa, and Arizona City. Its growing population includes a mix of longtime agricultural families, new residential communities, and a meaningful population of older adults who have relocated for Arizona's climate.

Banner Casa Grande Medical Center is the community's primary acute care hospital and the main source of hospice referrals for Casa Grande and Pinal County patients. Hospice agencies based in Phoenix or Tucson often extend their service areas to include Casa Grande, and some local or Pinal County-based agencies serve the community as well. When evaluating providers, confirm their familiarity with the Casa Grande area and their response times before committing.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Casa Grande patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost when a physician certifies a terminal prognosis of six months or less. Coverage includes nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program covers hospice for qualifying low-income Pinal County residents. Dual-eligible patients typically have all hospice costs covered. Arizona does not require a Certificate of Need for hospice providers, so Casa Grande families have access to a range of licensed agencies operating in the area.
