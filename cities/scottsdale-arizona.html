Finding Hospice Care in Scottsdale

Scottsdale is one of Arizona's most affluent communities and has a large population of retirees and part-year residents — including a significant snowbird population that winters here from colder states. Hospice and home care providers in Scottsdale serve a wide range of residential settings, from private homes in Arcadia and McCormick Ranch to senior living communities throughout north Scottsdale.

HonorHealth is the primary health system in Scottsdale, operating Scottsdale Shea Medical Center, Scottsdale Osborn Medical Center, and Scottsdale Thompson Peak Medical Center. Mayo Clinic Arizona's main campus sits on the Scottsdale/Phoenix border and operates a robust palliative care program. These institutions frequently coordinate hospice transitions for patients who have been under their care.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice care for eligible Scottsdale patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost when a physician certifies a terminal prognosis of six months or less. Covered services include nursing visits, comfort medications, medical equipment, home health aide support, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling for the family.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program covers hospice for qualifying low-income residents. Dual-eligible patients (Medicare + AHCCCS) typically have all hospice costs covered. Arizona does not require a Certificate of Need for hospice licensure, so Scottsdale families generally have a wide choice of both national agencies and locally owned providers.

Hospice Care in Scottsdale Retirement Communities

Scottsdale has a high density of assisted living facilities, independent living communities, and resort-style retirement campuses. Many hospice agencies operating in Scottsdale are experienced at providing care within these settings — working alongside facility staff to manage medications, equipment, and family communication.

If your loved one resides in a Scottsdale senior living community, ask the facility's care coordinator and the hospice agency whether they have an existing working relationship. Smooth communication between the facility and the hospice team is essential for consistent care quality.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find hospice care in Scottsdale?

Enter your Scottsdale ZIP code on BestHospice.com to view verified providers at no cost. Most agencies serve all of Scottsdale including north Scottsdale, Arcadia, Old Town, and nearby communities.

Can a snowbird enroll in hospice in Scottsdale?

Yes. Medicare hospice eligibility is not tied to primary state of residence. Scottsdale providers are accustomed to working with part-year residents and can coordinate with out-of-state physicians for certification.
