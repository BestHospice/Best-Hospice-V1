Finding Hospice Care in Payson

Payson is the largest community in Gila County, situated in the Tonto National Forest at the base of the Mogollon Rim. At approximately 5,000 feet elevation, Payson's cooler summers and pine-covered landscape attract both year-round residents and summer escape visitors from the Phoenix metro. The community has a growing population of retirees who have relocated for the quality of life the Rim Country offers.

Payson Regional Medical Center, part of the Banner Health system, is the local acute care hospital. Because Payson's relatively small size and remote location mean fewer provider options than urban Arizona centers, families sometimes need to consider agencies based in the Phoenix metro that extend coverage to the Rim Country. When evaluating any provider, ask specifically about on-call nurse response times to Payson and how they handle urgent nighttime calls from this location.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Payson patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost. Coverage includes nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program covers hospice for qualifying low-income Gila County residents. Dual-eligible patients typically have all hospice costs covered. Arizona has no Certificate of Need requirement, though Payson's rural nature means provider selection may be more limited than in metro areas.

What should I ask a hospice agency about serving Payson?
