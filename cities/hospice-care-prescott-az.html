What Is Hospice Care?

Hospice care is specialized support for people facing terminal illness with a life expectancy of six months or less, where care goals shift to comfort, dignity, and quality of life.

  • Pain and symptom relief
  • 24/7 on-call nursing support
  • Related medications and equipment
  • Social work, spiritual care, and bereavement support

Palliative and Home Health Care in Prescott

Palliative care can begin at any stage of serious illness, while home health can provide skilled nursing and therapy in the home. Families often combine these services based on evolving needs.

In Arizona, personal care averages around $25/hour, and many skilled home health services are covered by Medicare when eligibility criteria are met.

Coverage in Arizona: Medicare, AHCCCS, ALTCS, and VA

Most hospice care is covered by Medicare Part A for eligible patients. AHCCCS and ALTCS may cover long-term home-based care for qualifying residents. Veterans may also access VA-covered hospice and home health benefits.
