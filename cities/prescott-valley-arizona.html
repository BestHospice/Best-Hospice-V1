Finding Hospice Care in Prescott Valley

Prescott Valley is the largest town in Yavapai County and forms the eastern edge of the Prescott metro area. Sitting at approximately 5,100 feet elevation in central Arizona, the Prescott area is a popular destination for retirees seeking cooler temperatures, outdoor recreation, and a small-town pace. The Prescott metro — including Prescott Valley, Prescott, Chino Valley, and Dewey-Humboldt — has a large and growing older adult population, creating steady demand for quality hospice and home care services.

Yavapai Regional Medical Center (YRMC) operates campuses in both Prescott and Prescott Valley and is the primary acute care hospital for Yavapai County. Its clinical team coordinates hospice transitions for patients who have completed curative treatment or for whom comfort care is the best path forward. Hospice agencies serving the Prescott area include both locally based providers and Phoenix-area agencies that extend their coverage into the mountain communities.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Prescott Valley patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost. Coverage includes nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program and ALTCS long-term care program cover hospice for qualifying low-income Yavapai County residents. Dual-eligible patients typically have all hospice costs covered. Arizona has no Certificate of Need requirement, so the Prescott metro has multiple licensed agencies to choose from.
