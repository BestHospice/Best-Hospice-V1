What Is Hospice Care?

Hospice care is comfort-focused care for individuals with a terminal illness and an expected prognosis of six months or less. Rather than curative treatment, hospice prioritizes pain control, symptom relief, dignity, and family support.

  • 24/7 access to nurse support
  • Pain and symptom management
  • Home visits, medications, and equipment coordination
  • Social work and caregiver support
  • Bereavement support after loss

Palliative and Home Health Care in North Scottsdale

Palliative care can begin earlier in a serious illness and may be provided alongside treatment. Home health services deliver skilled nursing and therapy in the home when medically indicated. Together, these services can help reduce ER visits and improve quality of life for both patients and families.

In Arizona, personal home care averages about $25/hour, while many skilled home health services are covered by Medicare when eligibility requirements are met.

Coverage in Arizona

Medicare

Medicare Part A generally covers hospice for eligible patients and may cover qualifying home health services. Coverage often includes nursing care, medications related to terminal diagnosis, equipment, and counseling.

AHCCCS and ALTCS

Arizona Medicaid (AHCCCS) and ALTCS may provide long-term home-based support for eligible residents, depending on functional and financial criteria.
