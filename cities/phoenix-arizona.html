What Is Hospice Care?

Hospice is specialized medical care for people facing a terminal illness with a life expectancy of six months or less. Rather than pursuing curative treatment, hospice focuses entirely on comfort, dignity, and quality of life — for both the patient and everyone who loves them.

In Phoenix, hospice can be provided in a patient's own home, an assisted living or memory care facility, or a dedicated inpatient hospice unit. Every enrolled patient receives a full interdisciplinary team:

  • Pain and symptom management — including medications at no cost under Medicare
  • Registered nurses on scheduled visits and on-call 24 hours a day
  • Medical equipment delivered to the home (hospital bed, wheelchair, oxygen)
  • Home health aides assisting with personal care and bathing
  • Social workers for care coordination and family support
  • Chaplain or spiritual care tailored to the patient's beliefs
  • Bereavement counseling for families up to 13 months after the patient passes

Most hospice care in Arizona is covered at little or no out-of-pocket cost through Medicare Part A, AHCCCS (Arizona Medicaid), VA benefits, or private insurance.

Guidance for Families

When Should You Consider Hospice?

The most common thing families say after starting hospice is that they wish they had started sooner. Choosing hospice is not giving up — it is choosing a different kind of care, one that prioritizes presence, peace, and comfort over procedures and hospitalizations.

Consider requesting a hospice consultation when you notice any of the following:

  • A physician has estimated a life expectancy of six months or less
  • Two or more hospitalizations or ER visits in the past few months
  • Significant unintentional weight loss or declining appetite
  • Pain or symptoms that are increasingly difficult to control
  • Functional decline — needing help with three or more daily activities
  • The patient has expressed a wish to be at home rather than in a hospital
  • Caregiver exhaustion is affecting the whole family's wellbeing

A hospice consultation in Phoenix is free and carries no obligation to enroll. Most providers can complete an in-home assessment within 24 hours of your call.

Hospice vs. Palliative vs. Home Care
