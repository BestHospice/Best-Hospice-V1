Finding Hospice Care in Gilbert, AZ

Gilbert has grown from a small farming community into one of the largest and most populous towns in the United States. Its population has grown rapidly over the past two decades, and while it skews younger than many Arizona cities, it has a growing base of older residents whose families need hospice and home care services. Hospice providers serving Gilbert typically cover the full Southeast Valley, including Chandler, Queen Creek, Ahwatukee, and adjacent Mesa neighborhoods.

Banner Gateway Medical Center, located in Gilbert, is the area's primary acute care hospital and includes a Banner MD Anderson Cancer Center affiliation — making it a significant referral source for patients with cancer diagnoses who may be transitioning to comfort care. The palliative care team at Banner Gateway regularly coordinates with hospice agencies when patients are ready to shift their care goals.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice care for eligible Gilbert patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost when a physician certifies a terminal prognosis of six months or less. Covered services include nursing visits, comfort medications, medical equipment delivered to the home, aide services, social work, chaplain visits, and bereavement counseling for the family.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program covers hospice for qualifying low-income residents. Dual-eligible patients have most or all costs covered. Arizona does not require a Certificate of Need for hospice providers, so Gilbert families have a wide choice of agencies ranging from large national organizations to smaller independent teams.
