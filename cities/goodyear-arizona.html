Finding Hospice Care in Goodyear, AZ

Goodyear is one of Arizona's fastest-growing cities, with rapid residential development stretching west from Avondale toward Buckeye. While Goodyear's population skews younger than established Phoenix-area cities, it has a growing community of older residents and families who need hospice and home care resources. Providers serving Goodyear typically cover the full Southwest Valley, including Avondale, Litchfield Park, Buckeye, and southwest Phoenix neighborhoods.

Abrazo West Campus (previously known as West Valley Hospital) is the primary acute care hospital in Goodyear and Avondale. Banner Estrella Medical Center in southwest Phoenix also serves many patients from the Goodyear area. Both have clinical teams who coordinate hospice transitions when patients have reached a point where comfort-focused care best serves their goals.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Goodyear patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost. Covered services include nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program and the ALTCS long-term care system cover hospice for qualifying low-income residents. Dual-eligible patients typically have all costs covered. Arizona has no Certificate of Need for hospice licensure, so Goodyear families can choose from a broad range of agencies operating in the West Valley.
