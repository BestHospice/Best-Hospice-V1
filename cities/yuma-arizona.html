Finding Hospice Care in Yuma, AZ

Yuma is Arizona's third-largest metro area and the hub of the southwestern corner of the state, sitting near the California and Mexico borders. The city is well known as a major winter visitor destination — Yuma's population swells significantly from October through April each year as tens of thousands of retirees and snowbirds arrive from colder states and Canadian provinces. This creates both a large demand for hospice services in the winter months and a need for providers experienced with part-year residents.

Yuma's permanent community includes a large military presence (Marine Corps Air Station Yuma), a diverse year-round population, and an extensive agricultural sector. Yuma Regional Medical Center is the primary hospital and the main source of hospice referrals for Yuma County. Hospice agencies serving Yuma range from local independent providers to regional organizations operating across southwestern Arizona.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage in Yuma

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Yuma patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost when a physician certifies a terminal prognosis of six months or less. Coverage includes nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program and ALTCS long-term care program cover hospice for qualifying low-income Yuma County residents. Dual-eligible patients typically have all hospice costs covered. Arizona has no Certificate of Need requirement, so Yuma families have a choice of licensed hospice agencies.

Winter visitors enrolled in Medicare Advantage plans from their home state should confirm their plan's coverage for out-of-state hospice services. Traditional Medicare (Parts A and B) generally covers hospice regardless of where in the United States care is received.
