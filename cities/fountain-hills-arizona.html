Finding Hospice Care in Fountain Hills

Fountain Hills is a planned community in the northeast Phoenix metro, bordered by the McDowell Mountain Regional Park, the Fort McDowell Yavapai Nation, and the Scottsdale city limits. Known for its iconic fountain — one of the tallest in the world — Fountain Hills has a significant population of retirees and snowbirds who choose it for its desert scenery, quiet pace, and proximity to Scottsdale amenities.

Because Fountain Hills does not have an acute care hospital within its boundaries, residents primarily use HonorHealth facilities in north Scottsdale and the Mayo Clinic Arizona campus for hospital-based care. Hospice providers serving Fountain Hills are typically based in Scottsdale or northeast Phoenix and route their care teams into the community. When evaluating agencies, confirm response time to Fountain Hills and after-hours on-call coverage — the community's relative distance from central Phoenix means staffing response times can vary by agency.

Medicare and AHCCCS Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Fountain Hills patients at little to no out-of-pocket cost when a physician certifies a terminal prognosis of six months or less. Coverage includes nursing visits, comfort medications, home-delivered medical equipment, aide services, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Arizona's AHCCCS Medicaid program covers hospice for qualifying low-income residents. Dual-eligible patients (Medicare + AHCCCS) typically have all hospice costs covered. Arizona has no Certificate of Need, allowing a wide range of licensed providers to serve the Fountain Hills area.
