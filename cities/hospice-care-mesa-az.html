What Is Hospice Care?

Hospice care is specialized medical support for people with a terminal illness and a life expectancy of six months or less. Hospice focuses on comfort, dignity, symptom relief, and family support rather than curative treatment.

Hospice care in Mesa may be delivered at home, in assisted living, memory care, or in an inpatient setting depending on clinical needs.

  • Pain management and symptom control
  • 24/7 on-call nursing support
  • Medical equipment and medications related to terminal diagnosis
  • Social work, spiritual support, and bereavement care
  • Care coordination for families and caregivers

Palliative Care in Mesa, Arizona

Palliative care can start at any stage of a serious illness and can be provided alongside curative treatment. It helps manage pain, fatigue, breathlessness, anxiety, and other symptoms while improving quality of life.

Arizona has 20+ community palliative programs and 23 CAPC-affiliated organizations statewide, giving families in Mesa multiple care pathways across hospital, clinic, and home settings.

Home Health Care in Mesa, AZ

Home health care provides skilled nursing and therapy in the home when medically necessary. In Arizona, personal home care averages around $25/hour, while skilled care is often covered by Medicare with physician certification.

For eligible families, AHCCCS and ALTCS may cover long-term home-based services. This allows many seniors in Mesa to remain at home safely with professional support.

How Hospice and Home Care Are Covered in Arizona

Medicare

Medicare Part A covers hospice for patients with a prognosis of six months or less and includes nursing, related medications, equipment, and family support services. Medicare can also cover qualifying skilled home health episodes.

AHCCCS and ALTCS

AHCCCS is Arizona Medicaid. ALTCS supports long-term home and community-based services for eligible residents with functional and financial need.

Private Pay and Medicare Advantage

Many Medicare Advantage plans in Maricopa County include expanded benefits. Non-covered personal care is typically private pay, with long-term care insurance and VA benefits as additional funding sources.
