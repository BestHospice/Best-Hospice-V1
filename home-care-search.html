What Home Care Includes

Home care helps people remain safely and comfortably at home by providing non-medical daily living support. It is distinct from hospice and can complement other medical care your loved one is receiving.

Home care is appropriate at any stage of aging or illness — it does not require a terminal diagnosis.

Home Care Services Typically Include

  • Bathing, grooming, and personal hygiene assistance
  • Meal preparation and nutritional support
  • Medication reminders (non-medical)
  • Light housekeeping and laundry
  • Companionship and social engagement
  • Transportation to appointments and errands
  • Overnight and 24-hour care options
  • Respite care for family caregivers

Common Questions About Home Care

How do I find home care near me?

Enter your ZIP code above to instantly view verified home care providers in your area. Results are free, instant, and require no account or personal information upfront.

What is the difference between home care and home health care?

Home care typically refers to non-medical support — bathing, meals, companionship, and daily living assistance. Home health care involves skilled medical services like nursing visits, physical therapy, or wound care, and usually requires a physician's order. Both can be found through BestHospice.com.

Does Medicare cover home care?

Medicare covers skilled home health care (nursing, physical therapy) when ordered by a physician for homebound patients. Non-medical home care — companion care, personal care aides — is generally not covered by Medicare but may be covered by Medicaid, long-term care insurance, or veterans' benefits.

How much does home care cost?

Home care costs vary by location, hours needed, and provider. Non-medical home care typically ranges from $20–$35 per hour. Many families use Medicaid, long-term care insurance, or veterans' benefits to offset costs. See our full home care cost guide →

Is it free to search for home care providers?

Yes — completely free for families. Providers pay to be listed on BestHospice.com, so families always search at no cost and with no referral fees.
