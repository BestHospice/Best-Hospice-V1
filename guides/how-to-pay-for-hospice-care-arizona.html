How to Pay for Hospice Care in Arizona

Arizona-specific guidance for families making hospice, palliative, and home care decisions.

Find Verified Providers

This guide is built for Arizona families seeking clear, practical information. BestHospice.com is free for families and does not charge referral commissions.

Most Families Pay Little or Nothing

The biggest barrier is usually awareness, not eligibility. Medicare, AHCCCS/ALTCS, VA benefits, and private insurance often cover most hospice services.

Medicare Part A

Covers hospice nursing, medications tied to terminal diagnosis, equipment, aide services, counseling, and bereavement support for eligible patients.

AHCCCS and ALTCS

Arizona Medicaid pathways may cover hospice and long-term care support for eligible residents, including dual-eligible Medicare beneficiaries.

VA and Medicare Advantage

Veterans may receive VA-covered hospice benefits. Medicare Advantage plans must cover hospice and may include additional support options.

Private Pay and Other Support

Long-term care insurance, nonprofit charity programs, and veteran assistance resources can help cover non-insurance expenses.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is hospice free with Medicare?

For eligible patients, most hospice services are covered with minimal out-of-pocket costs.

Does ALTCS cover hospice in Arizona?

It may for eligible members through AHCCCS pathways.

Do veterans pay for hospice care?

Eligible veterans often receive covered care at no copay.

What if I do not have insurance?

Some providers and nonprofits offer charity or sliding-scale options.
