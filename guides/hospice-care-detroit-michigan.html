Hospice Care in the Detroit Metro

Metro Detroit — spanning Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties — is one of the largest metropolitan areas in the Midwest, with a well-developed hospice sector serving communities from the urban core to the outer suburbs of Troy, Rochester Hills, Novi, and beyond. The region has a large senior population and strong community ties to home-based care, with most families preferring to keep loved ones at home during the final chapter of life.

BestHospice.com helps Detroit-area families find partner hospice providers by ZIP code — so you can identify agencies that are actively staffed in your community, not just licensed for the state.

Detroit's Major Health Systems and Hospice Transitions

Henry Ford Health is one of the largest integrated health systems in the United States and the dominant academic medical system in Detroit. Henry Ford Hospital (New Center) is the flagship, with additional hospitals in West Bloomfield, Jackson, and the surrounding metro. Henry Ford has a comprehensive palliative care and hospice program that coordinates end-of-life transitions across its vast network.

Corewell Health East (formerly Beaumont Health) operates hospitals across Oakland and Macomb counties, including flagship campuses in Royal Oak and Troy. Corewell's palliative care teams coordinate hospice transitions for patients across the northern suburbs. Ascension Michigan operates hospitals under the St. John Providence brand across Wayne, Macomb, and Oakland counties, with a focus on Catholic mission-aligned healthcare.

Detroit Medical Center (DMC, Tenet Health) operates several hospitals in the city of Detroit including Detroit Receiving Hospital and Sinai Grace Hospital. DMC's social work teams coordinate hospice for complex patients in Detroit's urban neighborhoods. Michigan Medicine (University of Michigan Health System) in Ann Arbor — roughly 45 minutes from Detroit — is Michigan's premier academic medical center and coordinates hospice transitions for complex oncology and cardiac patients across southeast Michigan.

Dearborn and its surrounding communities have a large Arab American population — one of the largest concentrations in the United States. Hospice providers serving this corridor may offer Arabic-language services; confirm language capabilities when evaluating agencies.

Medicare and Michigan Medicaid Coverage in Detroit

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Detroit-area patients with a physician-certified prognosis of six months or less. Covered services include nursing visits, comfort medications, medical equipment, home health aide, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling — most at $0 to the patient or family.

Michigan Medicaid covers hospice through the Healthy Michigan Plan (low-income adults) and MI Health Link (dual-eligible individuals). Managed care plans serving the Detroit metro include Aetna Better Health of Michigan, Blue Cross Complete, Meridian Health Plan (Centene), Molina Healthcare of Michigan, Priority Health Choice, and United Health Plan of the River Valley. Dual-eligible patients can coordinate Medicare and Medicaid benefits to minimize cost.

Michigan licenses hospice providers through the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services (MDHHS). All Medicare-certified Michigan hospice providers meet state licensing requirements as a condition of federal certification.

How to Choose a Hospice Provider in Detroit

  • Do they serve your specific ZIP code? Coverage zones vary across Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb counties. Confirm active service before scheduling intake.
  • How quickly can they respond? Especially important for hospital discharges from Henry Ford, Beaumont, or DMC facilities.
  • What is their experience with your loved one's diagnosis? Cancer, dementia, heart failure, and COPD each require specific symptom management expertise.
  • Do they offer multilingual services? Detroit's diverse communities — Arab American, African American, Latino, Polish — may benefit from providers with specific language capabilities.
  • Do they have an inpatient arrangement? Confirm where inpatient care would be provided if symptoms escalate.
