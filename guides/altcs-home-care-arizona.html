ALTCS and Home Care Coverage in Arizona

Arizona-specific guidance for families making hospice, palliative, and home care decisions.

Find Verified Providers

This guide is built for Arizona families seeking clear, practical information. BestHospice.com is free for families and does not charge referral commissions.

What ALTCS Is

ALTCS is Arizona's long-term care Medicaid program under AHCCCS, designed for seniors and adults with disabilities who need nursing-home level care.

What ALTCS Covers

Home-based personal care, respite, therapies, caregiver training, medical supplies, and hospice for eligible members.

Eligibility Basics

Eligibility includes clinical need plus financial criteria. The published 2024 monthly income threshold is commonly referenced at $2,829.

What ALTCS Does Not Cover

Room and board in assisted living is generally not covered as a direct benefit; care services may be covered.

Coordination With Medicare

For dual-eligible members, Medicare pays first for covered services and ALTCS may fill gaps across long-term support needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ALTCS?

Arizona Long Term Care System for long-term Medicaid services and supports.

Does ALTCS cover home care?

Yes, for eligible members needing qualifying long-term support.

How do I apply for ALTCS?

Contact AHCCCS/ALTCS and complete financial and functional screening.

Does ALTCS also cover hospice?

Yes, for eligible members when clinical criteria are met.
