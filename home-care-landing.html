What home care includes

Home care helps people stay safely and comfortably in their own home.

•Help with bathing, dressing, and grooming
•Meal preparation and nutrition support
•Companionship and social engagement
•Light housekeeping and errands
•Medication reminders
•Transportation to appointments
•Skilled nursing visits when needed
•Respite care for family caregivers

Not sure if you need home care or hospice care?

Many families start searching for "home care" and discover their loved one may benefit from hospice care. We can help with both — at no cost to you.

Home Care

Daily living support for anyone who needs help at home — bathing, meals, companionship. No medical diagnosis required.

Hospice Care

Specialized comfort care for those with a terminal diagnosis. Covered by Medicare. Focuses on quality of life and family support.

Common Questions About Home Care

What is home care?

Home care helps people stay safely in their own home by providing support with daily activities like bathing, dressing, meals, and companionship. It is available to anyone who needs assistance at home, regardless of age or diagnosis.

How do I find home care near me?

Enter your ZIP code on BestHospice.com to instantly view verified home care providers in your area. Results are free, instant, and require no account or personal information upfront.

Does Medicare cover home care?

Medicare covers some home health services when ordered by a doctor — including skilled nursing and therapy. Non-medical daily living assistance is typically not covered by Medicare but may be covered by Medicaid or long-term care insurance.

What is the difference between home care and hospice care?

Home care focuses on daily living support. Hospice care is specialized comfort care for those near the end of life. Some families transition from home care to hospice as needs change. BestHospice.com can help you find the right type of care for your situation.
