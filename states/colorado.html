Finding Hospice and Home Care in Colorado

Colorado families value independence and quality of life deeply — values that align closely with the hospice philosophy of comfort-focused, patient-directed care. From the Front Range communities of Fort Collins and Longmont to the mountain towns beyond, Coloradans often seek providers who respect a loved one's wish to remain at home and maintain dignity through the final chapter of life.

Best Hospice and Home Health makes it easier to find and compare verified hospice and home care providers across Colorado's cities and regions. Use the city links below to view local options and contact agencies directly.

Medicare and Medicaid Information in Colorado

Medicare covers hospice in Colorado for beneficiaries who meet terminal illness eligibility requirements. Colorado's Medicaid program, Health First Colorado, also provides hospice coverage for qualifying enrollees and supports additional home-based services for those who need them. Private insurance plans vary. Colorado providers can confirm coverage specifics and help families understand their options before care begins.
