Finding Hospice Care in Utah

Utah's hospice market is concentrated along the Wasatch Front — the urban corridor stretching from Ogden in the north through Salt Lake City and down to Provo and Utah County in the south, which is home to more than 80% of the state's population. Spanish Fork, Orem, West Jordan, Sandy, and West Valley City are all within this corridor. More rural areas in southern Utah, including St. George and the surrounding communities, have fewer provider options but still have access to hospice through agencies serving broader geographic regions.

Hospice Care Along the Wasatch Front

Salt Lake City is anchored by University of Utah Health, one of the Mountain West's premier academic medical centers, with strong palliative care and oncology programs. Intermountain Health (formerly Intermountain Healthcare) — one of the largest nonprofit health systems in the country — operates hospitals and clinics across the Wasatch Front including Intermountain Medical Center in Murray, LDS Hospital, and McKay-Dee Hospital in Ogden. Both systems have palliative care teams that coordinate hospice transitions.

Utah County (Provo, Orem, Spanish Fork, West Jordan) is one of the fastest-growing counties in the United States. Utah Valley Hospital (Intermountain) and American Fork Hospital are the primary referral centers for this corridor. The area's large family sizes and strong community networks often mean that family caregivers are deeply involved in hospice care at home — a dynamic that hospice providers in Utah County are well-practiced in supporting.

Medicare and Utah Medicaid Coverage

Medicare Part A covers hospice for eligible Utah patients with a physician-certified prognosis of six months or less. Covered services include nursing visits, comfort medications related to the terminal diagnosis, medical equipment (hospital bed, oxygen, wheelchair), home health aide visits, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling — most at $0 to the patient or family.

Utah Medicaid covers hospice for qualifying low-income residents. Medicaid managed care is delivered through Healthy U (University of Utah Health Plans), Molina Healthcare of Utah, and SelectHealth (Intermountain). Dual-eligible patients can coordinate Medicare and Medicaid benefits. Medicare Advantage plans from SelectHealth, UnitedHealthcare, Humana, and Aetna are available in Utah and must cover hospice at Medicare Part A levels.

Utah licenses and regulates hospice providers through the Utah Department of Health and Human Services. All Medicare-certified Utah hospice providers meet state licensing requirements as a condition of federal certification.

Utah Hospice Laws, Licensing & Benefits

Certificate of Need (CON): Utah does not require a Certificate of Need for hospice licensure. Any provider that meets state licensing and Medicare certification requirements may operate in Utah, giving families broad provider choices along the Wasatch Front and in Utah County, Cache Valley, and other communities.

Advance Directive Form: Utah uses the Utah Advance Health Care Directive to document end-of-life preferences and designate a healthcare agent. Utah also uses the POLST (Physician Orders for Life-Sustaining Treatment) form as a portable medical order for patients with serious illness. Hospice teams can help families complete or update these documents when starting care.

Medicaid Home Care Programs: Utah Medicaid offers the Aging Waiver (also called the New Choices Waiver) for qualifying low-income adults who need home and community-based services as an alternative to nursing facility placement. Utah Medicaid managed care plans (Healthy U, Molina, SelectHealth) administer these services. Dual-eligible patients should confirm how their Utah Medicaid plan interacts with Medicare hospice benefits before enrolling.

Hospice Licensing Authority: Utah licenses hospice providers through the Utah Department of Health and Human Services, Office of Licensing. All Medicare-certified Utah hospice providers meet state licensing requirements as a condition of federal certification.

Free Medicare Counseling: Utah's SHIP program (administered through the Utah Department of Health and Human Services, Division of Aging and Adult Services) offers free Medicare counseling statewide. SHIP counselors can help families understand hospice benefits, Utah Medicaid waiver interaction with hospice, and Medicare Advantage plans from SelectHealth, Humana, and UnitedHealthcare across the Wasatch Front.
