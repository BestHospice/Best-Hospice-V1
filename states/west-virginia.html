Finding Hospice and Home Care in West Virginia

West Virginia families often face unique challenges in accessing hospice and home care, as the state's mountainous terrain and rural character mean that provider reach does not always match where people actually live. In a state where community bonds run deep and neighbors look after one another, finding a provider willing to travel to a patient's home — rather than requiring a move to a facility — can be genuinely life-changing.

Best Hospice and Home Health helps West Virginia families locate verified providers, understand service coverage in their area, and connect directly with agencies. Use the city links below to explore hospice and home care options near you.

Medicare and Medicaid Information in West Virginia

Medicare covers hospice for eligible West Virginia residents when a terminal illness is physician-certified. WV Medicaid also covers hospice services for qualifying enrollees and can support additional home-based care needs. Given the state's geography, some providers serve large rural service areas, and coverage details can vary. Local providers can confirm what is included under your plan before services begin.
