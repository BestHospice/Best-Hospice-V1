Medicare and Medicaid Coverage in Alabama

Medicare Part A covers hospice services for eligible Alabama patients when a physician certifies a terminal prognosis of six months or less. Covered services typically include nursing visits, comfort medications tied to the terminal diagnosis, medical equipment such as hospital beds and oxygen, home health aide support, social work, chaplain services, and bereavement counseling.

Alabama Medicaid (through Alabama Medicaid Agency) can also cover hospice for qualifying low-income patients and may fund home health aide services separately. Private insurance coverage varies by plan.

Any Alabama provider listed on BestHospice.com can clarify what your specific coverage includes and handle eligibility verification before care begins.

Alabama Hospice Laws, Licensing & Benefits

Certificate of Need (CON): Alabama requires a Certificate of Need for hospice licensure. This means the number of licensed hospice providers per service area is regulated by the state — families in some areas may have fewer choices than in non-CON states. Confirm that the provider holds a current Alabama CON before enrolling.

Advance Directive Form: Alabama uses the Alabama Advance Directive for Health Care to document end-of-life care preferences. The state also recognizes POLST (Physician Orders for Life-Sustaining Treatment) as a portable medical order. Hospice teams can help families complete or update these documents when starting care.

Medicaid Home Care Waiver: Alabama Medicaid offers the Elderly and Disabled (E&D) Waiver, which provides home and community-based services to qualifying low-income seniors and people with disabilities as an alternative to nursing facility placement. Contact the Alabama Medicaid Agency to determine eligibility.

Hospice Licensing Authority: Alabama licenses hospice providers through the Alabama Department of Public Health (ADPH). All Medicare-certified Alabama hospice providers meet state licensing requirements as a condition of federal certification.

Free Medicare Counseling: Alabama's SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) offers free, unbiased Medicare counseling to help families understand hospice benefits, Medicare Advantage plans, and supplemental coverage options. Call 1-800-MEDICARE or contact the Alabama Department of Senior Services to connect with a SHIP counselor.
