Finding Hospice and Home Care in Florida

Florida has one of the largest and most active hospice markets in the United States, driven by a substantial senior population and a long tradition of community-based end-of-life care. The state spans major metro areas like Miami, Tampa, Orlando, and Jacksonville, alongside retirement-heavy communities throughout the Gulf Coast and Atlantic coast where demand for quality hospice and home care is especially high.

Medicare and Medicaid in Florida

Medicare covers hospice in Florida for eligible beneficiaries with a physician-certified terminal diagnosis and a prognosis of six months or less. Florida Medicaid also covers hospice services for qualifying enrollees, and Florida's Statewide Medicaid Managed Care Long-Term Care program covers home- and community-based services including personal care and aide support.

Florida has a high concentration of Medicare Advantage plan enrollees — if your family member is on a Medicare Advantage plan, ask the hospice provider to confirm in-network status before enrolling. Most plans provide equivalent hospice benefits to traditional Medicare.

Hospice Care in Florida: What Families Should Know

Florida hospice providers are licensed by the Agency for Health Care Administration (AHCA). Families can verify a provider's license and any inspection history through the AHCA health facility database. Medicare-certified hospices in Florida are also subject to federal Conditions of Participation and CMS quality reporting.

Florida law allows a family member or legal representative to make hospice decisions on behalf of a patient who lacks decision-making capacity. If no healthcare surrogate has been designated by advance directive, Florida's health care surrogate statute establishes a priority order for who can authorize care decisions.
