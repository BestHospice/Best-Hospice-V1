Finding Hospice and Home Care in South Carolina

South Carolina's warm coastal communities and tight-knit inland towns share a family-first culture that extends naturally into how residents approach end-of-life care. Whether a family lives in the Charleston area, the Midlands, or the Upstate, the priority is often the same: finding a compassionate provider who will meet a loved one where they are and honor their wishes.

Best Hospice and Home Health helps South Carolina families locate verified hospice and home care providers, compare local coverage, and contact agencies without the pressure of a sales process. Use the city links below to find providers in your area.

Medicare and Medicaid Information in South Carolina

Medicare covers hospice for eligible South Carolina residents when a physician certifies a terminal illness of six months or less. South Carolina's Medicaid program, SC Healthy Connections Medicaid, also covers hospice for qualifying enrollees and may fund additional home and personal care services. Providers in South Carolina can review coverage details with families and confirm what is included before care begins.
