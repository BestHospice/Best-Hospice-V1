Finding Hospice and Home Care in California

California is home to one of the most diverse and populous hospice markets in the country, with provider options spanning urban medical centers, suburban neighborhoods, and remote rural communities. Families navigating end-of-life care in California often encounter a large and sometimes overwhelming number of agencies, making clear, side-by-side comparison especially valuable.

Best Hospice and Home Health helps California families identify verified local providers, understand which services are available in their area, and connect with agencies directly. Use the city links below to explore hospice and home care options near you.

Medicare and Medicaid Information in California

Medicare covers hospice for eligible California residents with a certified terminal prognosis. California's Medicaid program, Medi-Cal, covers hospice services for qualifying individuals and also funds many home and community-based care options. For dual-eligible patients, Medi-Cal and Medicare can work in tandem. Providers can walk families through exactly what is covered under their plan before any care begins.
