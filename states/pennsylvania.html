Finding Hospice and Home Care in Pennsylvania

Pennsylvania's mix of dense suburban communities, mid-size cities, and rural stretches means the hospice and home care experience can look very different depending on where a family lives. In the Philadelphia suburbs — from Coatesville to Malvern to Royersford — families often have strong provider options and benefit from being able to compare agencies side by side before making a decision under emotional stress.

Best Hospice and Home Health helps Pennsylvania families find verified providers in their city, understand service coverage, and connect with local agencies directly. Use the city links below to explore options near you.

Medicare and Medicaid Information in Pennsylvania

Medicare covers hospice in Pennsylvania for beneficiaries with a certified terminal prognosis. Pennsylvania's Medicaid program, known as Medical Assistance (MA), covers hospice services for qualifying low-income individuals and can fund additional home-based supports. Dual-eligible patients may access both programs. Pennsylvania providers can review your plan details and explain covered services before care begins.
