Finding Hospice and Home Care in Ohio

Ohio has a strong tradition of community-based hospice care, with well-established providers serving major metros including Columbus, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Dayton, and Akron, as well as smaller communities throughout the state. Ohio is consistently among the top states for hospice utilization, with a high percentage of Medicare beneficiaries receiving hospice care at end of life.

Best Hospice and Home Health helps Ohio families compare verified providers by city, review coverage areas, and connect with local agencies directly. Use the search tool to find hospice and home care support near you.

Medicare and Medicaid in Ohio

Medicare covers hospice in Ohio for eligible beneficiaries with a physician-certified terminal diagnosis and a prognosis of six months or less. Ohio Medicaid also covers hospice services for qualifying enrollees. Ohio's PASSPORT program and MyCare Ohio Managed Care plans provide home- and community-based services for older adults and individuals with disabilities.

Ohio Medicaid enrollees in a managed care plan should confirm with the hospice provider how enrollment affects their current coverage. Hospice typically shifts primary billing to the Medicare Hospice Benefit, with Medicaid covering Medicare cost-sharing and some services not covered by Medicare.

Hospice Care in Ohio: What Families Should Know

Ohio hospice providers are licensed by the Ohio Department of Health and subject to state and federal certification requirements. Families can review provider survey results through the Ohio Department of Health website and through Medicare's Care Compare tool at Medicare.gov.

Ohio law allows individuals to designate a healthcare power of attorney to make medical decisions when they are unable to do so. Living wills and DNR orders are also valid in Ohio. The hospice team's social worker can help families navigate advance directive documents and ensure the care plan reflects the patient's wishes.
