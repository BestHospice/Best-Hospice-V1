Finding Hospice and Home Care in Texas

Texas is one of the largest and most active hospice markets in the country, with a provider landscape that spans enormous metro areas like Houston and Dallas, rapidly growing suburbs, and smaller communities like Rhome and Shenandoah. The sheer scale of the state means families benefit greatly from a straightforward way to identify which local agencies serve their specific zip code.

Best Hospice and Home Health helps Texas families compare verified providers by city, review coverage areas, and connect with local agencies directly. Use the city links below to find hospice and home care support near you.

Medicare and Medicaid Information in Texas

Medicare covers hospice in Texas for eligible beneficiaries with a certified terminal diagnosis. Texas Medicaid, including the STAR+PLUS managed care program for individuals with long-term care needs, also covers hospice and home-based services for qualifying enrollees. Coverage specifics vary by managed care organization. Texas providers can confirm what your plan covers and guide families through the enrollment process.
