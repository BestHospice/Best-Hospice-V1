Provider Billing

Subscription plans and billing terms for hospice, palliative, and home care providers using Best Hospice.

Overview

Best Hospice is a technology platform that connects families to licensed providers. This page outlines subscription plans and billing terms for providers who want access to the Best Hospice marketplace.

What providers are paying for

Subscriptions pay for platform access, marketing exposure, and inbound leads. Payments are not for medical services or patient care.

Important: Best Hospice is not a healthcare provider, does not provide medical care, and does not bill insurance. Subscriptions are for platform access and marketing services only.

Payments are processed securely by Stripe.

How Verified, Featured, and Priority work

Each tier includes all benefits from the level below, with more visibility and placement as you move up.

Featured and Priority members are notified earlier when new leads come in, as we allocate more bandwidth to higher‑tier memberships.

  • ⭐ Verified ($250/mo) — Identity + location verification, a Verified badge, and standard placement in local searches.
  • ⭐⭐ Featured ($375/mo) — Verified plus boosted visibility across your service radius and stronger placement in results.
  • ⭐⭐⭐ Priority ($500/mo) — Verified + Featured with top placement where applicable and maximum exposure.
