Why education matters when choosing care

Choosing between hospice, palliative, and home care is one of the most important decisions a family can make — often under significant time pressure and emotional stress. Understanding the differences, costs, and eligibility criteria before a crisis hits gives families the clarity and confidence to act quickly when it matters most.

Our guides are written in plain language, reviewed for clinical accuracy, and updated regularly. They are designed to answer the questions families are actually asking — not the questions providers wish they were asking.

Most common questions families ask

  • When is it time for hospice? — Learn the signs that hospice may be appropriate and how to start the conversation with your family and doctor.
  • What does Medicare pay for? — Medicare Part A covers hospice services in full for most patients. Understand what's included and what isn't.
  • What's the difference between hospice and palliative care? — Palliative care can begin at any stage of illness. Hospice is for patients with a six-month prognosis who choose comfort over curative treatment.
  • How do I choose a hospice provider? — Not all providers are equal. Learn what questions to ask and what verified means on BestHospice.com.
  • What does home care include? — Home care covers daily living assistance, companionship, and skilled nursing visits. It is distinct from hospice and can complement palliative services.
