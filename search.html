What is hospice home care?

Hospice home care delivers comfort-focused medical support directly to the patient's home. A team including nurses, aides, social workers, and chaplains visits regularly to manage pain, support daily needs, and help the family — covered by Medicare Part A for eligible patients.

What is the difference between hospice, palliative, and home care?

Hospice care is comfort-focused care for patients with a terminal prognosis of six months or less who have stopped curative treatment. Palliative care can begin at any stage of illness and runs alongside treatment, focusing on symptom relief and quality of life. Home care provides daily living support — bathing, meals, companionship — for people who need help at home but are not necessarily near end of life.
