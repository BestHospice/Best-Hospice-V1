Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2026

Clear, simple billing expectations for provider subscriptions.

Billing cadence

Provider subscriptions are billed monthly and auto-renew unless canceled.

Refunds

Subscriptions are generally non-refundable once a billing cycle begins. If you believe a billing error occurred, contact support@besthospice.com and we will review.

Cancellations

You may cancel at any time. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period, and service continues until that period ends.

How to cancel

Log into your Provider Dashboard and use the Billing section, or contact support@besthospice.com for assistance.
