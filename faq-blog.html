FAQs

  • How much does hospice cost? Medicare typically covers hospice services, and many private plans do as well.
  • Is hospice covered by insurance? Most patients use Medicare; providers can confirm coverage details for your plan.
  • Hospice vs palliative vs home health? Hospice is comfort-focused near end of life; palliative supports comfort during treatment; home health focuses on daily living support.
  • How fast can care begin? Many providers can start services within 24–48 hours after eligibility is confirmed.
